# Prism Tiles
Form beautiful gradients by rearranging the tiles. Try to do it in as few steps as possible to have a chance to claim your place on the Leaderboard!

There are 30+ puzzles in 4 chapters, good luck!

## Controls
Touch or mouse.

## Leaderboard
The game has a weekly leaderboard, it resets on every Sunday at 13:00 CET.
